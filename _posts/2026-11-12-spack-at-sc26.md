[Supercomputing 2026 (SC26)](https://sc26.supercomputing.org/) kicks off
next week in Chicago, and the Spack team will be there with a full-day
tutorial, a BOF panel, a happy hour, and our home base at the HPSF booth.
See below for the list of events.

Don't forget to grab some **Spack swag**! We'll have custom embroidered beanies for the cold Chicago weather at the tutorial, the happy hour, and possibly even the HPSF booth.

### Mon., November 16

* **8:30am - 5:00pm**, in W190b
  <br/>
  Join us for our full-day tutorial:
  [**Managing HPC Software Complexity with Spack**](https://sc26.conference-program.com/presentation/?id=tut184&sess=sess170).
  This is a full-day tutorial on using Spack, covering
  installing and authoring packages, environments, developer workflows,
  and deployment at HPC facilities.

### Tues., November 17

* **12:15pm - 1:15pm**, in W183a
  <br/>
  Spack will be part of a panel at the BOF
 [ **The Future of Python on HPC Systems**](https://sc26.conference-program.com/presentation/?id=bof140&sess=sess366).

### Wed., November 18

* **6:30pm - 9:30pm**
  <br/>
  Come to the **SC26 Spack Happy Hour**, sponsored by Susquehanna!
  [Register here](https://luma.com/susque-m0ab)

### All week

* Meet us at the [**HPSF Booth**](https://hpsf.io/event/sc26/).
* Join the **#sc26** channel on [Spack Slack](https://slack.spack.io) and
  meet up with others at the conference.

We're looking forward to seeing you all there!
