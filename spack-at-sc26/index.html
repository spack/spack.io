    Spack at SC26

    Spack will be at SC26 next week in Chicago! Check out the Spack lineup below, and grab a warm Spack hat while you’re there.

    On this page

    • Mon., November 16
    • Tues., November 17
    • Wed., November 18
    • All week

    Supercomputing 2026 (SC26) kicks off next week in Chicago, and the Spack team will be there with a full-day tutorial, a BOF panel, a happy hour, and our home base at the HPSF booth. See below for the list of events.

    Don’t forget to grab some Spack swag! We’ll have custom embroidered beanies for the cold Chicago weather at the tutorial, the happy hour, and possibly even the HPSF booth.

    Mon., November 16

    • 8:30am - 5:00pm, in W190b
      Join us for our full-day tutorial: Managing HPC Software Complexity with Spack. This is a full-day tutorial on using Spack, covering installing and authoring packages, environments, developer workflows, and deployment at HPC facilities.

    Tues., November 17

    • 12:15pm - 1:15pm, in W183a
      Spack will be part of a panel at the BOF The Future of Python on HPC Systems.

    Wed., November 18

    • 6:30pm - 9:30pm
      Come to the SC26 Spack Happy Hour, sponsored by Susquehanna! Register here

    All week

    • Meet us at the HPSF Booth.
    • Join the #sc26 channel on Spack Slack and meet up with others at the conference.

    We’re looking forward to seeing you all there!
